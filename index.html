Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free. You get every tool, your own professional page, booking, invoicing, and payments from day one. No trial period, no credit card, no catch. We make money by adding a small service fee on top of what clients pay. You always keep 100% of the rate you set.

What services can I offer?

Over 100 services across 14 categories. If people in Las Vegas need it done, there is a good chance it is already listed.

Do I need business experience?

Not at all. If you already clean houses, detail cars, walk dogs, or do any kind of service work, you have everything you need. HappyTask gives you the tools to handle the business side so you can focus on the work you're already good at.

What if I already have clients?

Even better. Send them your HappyTask link instead of texting back and forth. They book themselves, you get paid automatically, and you have a record of every job. It is the easiest upgrade to how you already work.

Do I need clients already to use HappyTask?

Nope. Whether you have 50 clients or zero, HappyTask gives you the tools to look professional, get booked, and get paid. If you want extra visibility, our Marketing Boost add-on gets you listed in search results so new clients can find you.

How does HappyTask make money?

You keep 100% of your rate. We add a small percentage on top of what the client pays, and we show you exactly what it is every time. No surprises. The more you grow and get booked, the better we do.

Will there be clients ready to book me?

HappyTask is built to help you run your business, not to send you unqualified leads. You bring your clients, your reputation, and your hustle. We give you the professional page, booking, and payments to back it up. If you want to reach new clients too, our Marketing Boost add-on gets you listed in search results where people are already looking.
